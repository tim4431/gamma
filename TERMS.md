# Gamma Cloud terms

_Last updated: 2026-11-08._

These terms cover Gamma Cloud: the account at
[account.gammapdf.com](https://account.gammapdf.com) and the paid plans
that add a hosted Gamma server. They are an agreement between you and the
developer of Gamma, referred to below as "we". The Gamma software itself
(the desktop app, the server you can host yourself, the browser extension)
is open source and is covered by its own license, not by these terms. How
your data is handled is described in the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

## The short version

- A Gamma Cloud account is free. A paid plan is a subscription to a
  library we keep running for you: an account on our shared server (Lite
  and Plus) or a Gamma server of your own (Pro).
- **Payments are not refunded.** You can cancel at any time, and the plan
  stays active until the end of the period you have paid for.
- Your library is yours. You can export all of it at any time, including
  after a plan ends.
- **Do not share content that harms others** (the list is under "What
  you may not share"). Anyone can report a comment, a shared page or an AI
  reply from its menu in the app.

## Your account

You need a confirmed e-mail address to use a Gamma Cloud account with a
Gamma server. Keep your sign-in details to yourself; you are responsible
for what is done with your account. You can delete the account on its
Settings page. Deleting it also deletes your library on the shared
server and takes down the pages you published there
([Deleting your Gamma Cloud account](DELETE_ACCOUNT.md)).

## Plans and payment

The plans, what each includes and their prices are listed on the
[pricing page](https://gammapdf.com/pricing). Prices are in US dollars;
tax is added where it applies.

A plan is paid in advance, by the month or by the year, and renews
automatically for the same period until you cancel it. Payments are
handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.

We may change a plan's price or contents. A change applies from your next
renewal, and we tell you by e-mail before it does.

## No refunds

Payments are not refunded, in whole or in part, including when you cancel
partway through a period, stop using the service, change to a cheaper
plan, or delete your account. The exception is where the law that applies
to you gives you a right to a refund that cannot be excluded; these terms
do not limit such a right.

A plan starts as soon as the payment is made. By subscribing you ask us
to start the service at once.

## Cancelling and changing a plan

You can cancel on the Plan and billing page of your account. Cancelling
stops the next renewal. The plan stays active until the end of the period
already paid for, and until then you can undo the cancellation there.

You can change to another plan or billing period at any time. The change
is confirmed on Stripe's page, which shows what is charged before you
agree.

Deleting your account ends a plan at once, without a refund.

## When a plan ends or a payment fails

If a renewal payment fails, your plan keeps working for 7 days while the
payment is retried. If it is still unpaid after that, the plan ends as
described here.

**Lite and Plus.** Your library is kept where it is, but it is closed: you
cannot sign in to it without a plan, so export what you want to keep
before the plan ends. Your account goes back to the free allowance, and
the pages you published stay online. We may delete files beyond the free
allowance 90 days after the plan ended, and tell you by e-mail before we
do. Subscribing again opens the library at once, as it was.

**Pro.** Your server:

1. becomes read-only at once. You and your members can still sign in, read
   everything and export it;
2. is stopped 30 days later;
3. is deleted, with its files and backups, 90 days after the plan ended.

Subscribing again before the deletion brings the server back as it was.
After the deletion the data cannot be recovered. A library on the shared
server and a Pro server are separate: moving between Plus and Pro does not
move your files, so export them from one and import them into the other.

- sexual content involving minors, of any kind, or sexual content shared

- violence, or content that encourages violence, self-harm or terrorism;
- spam, scams, malware, or content made to deceive people;
- illegal content, or material you have no right to share, such as

The AI chat in Gamma writes with the provider and the account you choose.
What it writes is yours to use as these rules allow: do not share its
output where you could not share the same text yourself.

## Reporting content and people

Every comment, shared or published page, and AI reply in the Gamma apps
has a **Report** action in its menu. A report goes to the people who run
that Gamma server, and, when the server uses Gamma Cloud, to us as well,
with what was reported, why, and who wrote it. We review the reports that
reach us and may remove the content, stop a share or a published page,
or suspend the account that posted it. On a server someone else runs,
its admin decides what happens on it. You can also write to
<support@gammapdf.com>.

We may suspend an account or a server that breaks these rules or endangers
the service. A suspended server is read-only, so its owner can still export
their data.

We work to keep the service running and your data safe, and we keep
backups. The service is nevertheless provided as it is, without a guarantee
that it will be uninterrupted or free of faults. Keep your own copy of
anything you cannot afford to lose; the export is there for that.

To the extent the law allows, our liability for any claim about the
service is limited to the amount you paid for it in the 12 months before
the claim.
