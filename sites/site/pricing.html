You pay for the server, not the app

Every feature of Gamma is in the free app. A plan adds a server that is always on; the storage figure is its cap, not the product.

Run it yourself instead

The same server is one Docker image you can host on your own machine or VPS, with no account with us. Self-hosting →

Change or cancel any time

Switch plans or cancel from your Gamma Cloud account. A cancelled server stays readable for 30 days so you can export everything.

Payments

Checkout, invoices and card details are handled by Stripe; Gamma never sees your card. Prices are in US dollars; tax is added where it applies.
