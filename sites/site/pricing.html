Is Gamma free?

Yes, free and open source, with no account. Every feature is in the free app; a plan adds a library that is always reachable.

Run it yourself instead

The same server is one Docker image, with no account with us. Self-hosting

Change or cancel any time

From your Gamma Cloud account. A cancelled plan runs to the end of the period you paid for. A Lite or Plus library is then closed; subscribe again to reopen it. Files beyond the free allowance may be deleted after 90 days, so export first. A Pro server stays readable for 30 days so you can export everything.

Payments

Stripe handles checkout, invoices and cards; Gamma never sees your card. Prices are in US dollars, with tax added where it applies. Payments are not refunded: see the terms.
